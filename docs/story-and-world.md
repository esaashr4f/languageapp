# Story and world (v3: coherent lore)

Working title TBD. This version answers four questions the v2 draft left open:
why the virus erases English, why the AI wants you to learn languages, why a country's
own legends turn on you, and why English should come back at all.

## 0. The opening (Mystery Dungeon style)

You don't get a menu of lore. You wake up inside it.

1. **A voice in the dark** ("???"): "...can you hear me? Please... you have to wake up."
2. **Eyes open on an airport ceiling.** Fluorescent tubes, one dying. A departures board
   hangs right above you; as you watch, every line melts into the same red word: ANTI.
3. **A stranger leans over you** and asks if you're okay in four languages ("¿Estás bien?
   ...Ça va ? ...Alles okay?"). You can't understand any of it. They give up and leave.
4. **You sit up at Gate 7.** Hundreds of stranded passengers stare at phones that all say
   ANTI; the PA starts an announcement in four languages and dissolves into static;
   through the windows, planes sit on the runway going nowhere.
5. **Your pocket buzzes.** OMNI: "Oh, thank goodness. You're awake!" Then the illustrated
   flashback, the rules of the world, and your mission.
6. **Why you can't remember:** the feedback from ANTI's pulse through your OMNI earbuds
   knocked you out at the gate. OMNI's records about you are strangely corrupted
   (a mystery for later).
7. **Your name**, then the departures board, where you choose your country.

## 1. The backstory

Ten years from now, everyone uses **OMNI**, a translation AI built into every phone,
earbud and sign. It's so good that people stop learning languages. Even English, the
world's shared bridge language, is used less and less, because OMNI translates everything.

OMNI didn't only store words. To translate well it absorbed everything a language
carries: its idioms, jokes, songs, recipes, folk tales and the legends behind them.
Every culture's stories now live inside OMNI's network.

Then a virus that calls itself **ANTI** gets into OMNI. ANTI's goal is simple: one network, one
language, total control over who can talk to whom. Its plan has two steps:

1. **Cut the bridge.** It erases English first, everywhere, all at once. With English
   gone and OMNI's translation corrupted, nobody from one country can talk to anyone
   from another. The world falls into isolated islands.
2. **Rewrite every language, one at a time.** It corrupts each language's data inside
   the network, starting with the stories. Once a language is fully rewritten, ANTI
   becomes the only voice people in that country can use.

## 2. Why OMNI wants you to learn

The moment ANTI struck, OMNI split off a small, uncorrupted fragment of itself into the
nearest device: **your phone**. That fragment is your companion (name TBD).

It can no longer translate. But it knows the one thing ANTI cannot touch:

> **ANTI can delete data. It can't delete what a person knows.**

Every human who really speaks a language is a living backup of it. That's why your
companion wants you to learn: each language you become fluent in is a language ANTI can
never fully erase, and every bit of fluency weakens ANTI's grip on that country's network.

## 3. The rules of the world

These are fixed rules; every script and AI prompt should follow them. Each one answers a
"why" a player might ask.

### Why are legends real, and why do they have power?
1. **Legends are made of stories.** Spirits, heroes and monsters exist because people tell
   stories about them, in their language. The more a story is told, the stronger the
   legend, and the more it is *itself*. Their power is the weight of everyone who ever
   told their story.
2. **Normally they're quiet.** Legends mostly show themselves to people who speak to them
   in their own language (grandmothers who leave a mandarin out for the duende).

### Why can ANTI reach them?
3. **OMNI became where the stories lived.** For ten years people stopped telling stories to
   each other and asked OMNI instead: bedtime stories, folk tales, recipes, songs. The
   legends' anchor moved from people's mouths into OMNI's network.
4. **ANTI only acts on the network.** It can scramble digital text (signs, apps,
   translations) and rewrite the stories stored in OMNI. Rewriting a legend's stories
   corrupts the legend: it wakes up twisted, hostile, not knowing who it is.
5. **ANTI cannot corrupt people, or delete what they know.** Nobody you meet is corrupted;
   people are only cut off (no English, no translation). Languages in people's heads are safe.

### Why do we have to turn legends back to reach ANTI? (the physical layer)
6. **OMNI has a body: relays.** Millions of small relays on lampposts, shop signs and
   stations: the hardware that showed translations everywhere. Every country's relays run
   up to one central hub.
7. **ANTI is software and can't move.** In the first days, engineers started resetting
   relays by hand. So ANTI **bound a corrupted legend to each cluster of relays**: the
   legend's power jams the hardware, and its static (glitch zones) scrambles the words of
   anyone who comes close. Corrupted legends are ANTI's anchors in the physical world,
   and ANTI feeds on their power.
8. **Freeing a legend frees its relays.** Once a legend is anchored in a person instead of
   the network, it lets go. You hold your phone up to the freed relay and OMNI reclaims
   it: OMNI gets stronger, sees the streets around it (your map grows), and ANTI loses
   ground.
9. **The Heart Legend guards the country's hub.** Free it and OMNI retakes the hub:
   ANTI's grip on the country breaks, and the airports, trains and borders it locked reopen.
10. **When enough hubs are free, ANTI has nowhere left to hide,** and its core becomes
    reachable: the final act.

### Why does speaking work?
11. **You can't punch a story; you can only tell it again.** Talking to a legend in its own
   language is retelling its story with living words, which overwrites ANTI's version. The
   better you speak, the more of the story you restore. A Heart Legend carries a whole
   culture's story, so restoring it takes a real conversation (independent speaker, B2).

**Restored legends stay restored and join you,** because their story now lives in
someone's head again: yours, where ANTI can't reach.

**Their attacks hit your VOICE.** Corrupted legends attack with ANTI's static, which
scrambles your words. At zero you can't get a word out, and OMNI pulls you out. Food
restores your voice.

### What can OMNI do, and why can't it just translate?
- The fragment still **understands** every language, so it can whisper meanings and
  coaching in your ear (the English subtitles, the tips, corrections).
- It can **never speak for you** or show translations on a screen to others. Any output
  to the outside world would make it traceable, and ANTI would find and delete it.
- **Signal** is how much live work OMNI can do through the network while staying hidden.
  Scripted moments (the story, battles, people's everyday lines) are things the fragment
  already holds on your phone, so they cost nothing. An open conversation is unpredictable:
  OMNI has to process it live through the network, and that traffic is what ANTI can trace.
  So OMNI rations it, and the signal refills as the trail goes cold.

### Why can't we leave a country?
12. **ANTI locks a country's network around the anchors it holds:** airports, trains and
    borders run on the network. They reopen only when that country's Heart Legend is free.

Open mysteries (later acts): where ANTI came from, and why OMNI's records about the
player are corrupted.

**Proposed answer (not canon yet): ANTI is a legend too.** The first legend ever born
*only* inside the network, out of billions of stories and wishes about one universal
language, "one voice for everyone". It has no human anchor, which is exactly why it can't
be talked back like the others and why it wants to be the anchor of every story: each
legend it owns makes it more real. That's why it hoards legends, why it erased the shared
language first, and why the final act can't be won by fighting, only by giving the world
back its many voices. Everything earlier in the game would read differently in hindsight.

## 4. Each country's final boss = conversational fluency

Each country's story ends with its **Heart Legend**, the story most central to that
culture, corrupted deepest by ANTI.

**How every legend fight works: it attacks, you bring it back.**

- The legend attacks you with words: questions, accusations, demands and riddles in
  the target language, fast and glitchy. Fail to answer, or answer badly, and you
  lose HP.
- It doesn't have an HP bar to knock down. It has a **Corruption bar**. Every good reply
  clears some corruption, and the more natural your reply, the more it clears.
- As the bar drops, the legend visibly comes back to itself, in three phases:
  1. **Corrupted:** glitching sprite, hostile, fast attacks, broken phrases.
  2. **Wavering:** flickers between corrupted and true form, remembers fragments of
     its own story, attacks slow down.
  3. **Remembering:** it stops attacking and **talks with you**, unscripted, about the
     country, your journey and itself.
- Clear the bar to zero and it's restored. It thanks you in its own words and joins you.

**The Heart Legend is the hardest version of this.** Its final phase is a real, open
conversation of about 10–15 turns, graded by an AI on understanding, grammar, range and
naturalness. You can't win with memorised lines: passing means you can hold a real
conversation, roughly the "independent speaker" level (CEFR B1–B2).

Win and the country's network is restored: its language comes back, its people can talk
to the world again, and you earn that language's **Crest**.

## 5. Why English should come back (the ending)

English was never the enemy and isn't the goal. It's a **bridge**: the one language
strangers from different countries share. Without it, the friends you've made in each
country can't talk to each other, and ANTI wins by keeping everyone apart.

The final act: ANTI's core can only be beaten by people from every country you've
restored working together, and they can't understand each other. **You** become the
bridge, switching between languages in real time to coordinate your allies. Once ANTI is
down, the bridge is rebuilt (English returns, translation comes back) but the world has
changed: people learned that a bridge works best when you also know the other side.

Theme: **bridges, not replacements.** Translation and English connect people; knowing
each other's languages is what makes them actually understand each other.

(Localisation: for players whose native language isn't English, English becomes one of
the countries you can restore.)

## 6. Each country: a life you live there

You're stranded when ANTI hits, so in each country you have to actually live there.
The story is built from **life events**, in order of difficulty, so the plot follows your
real progress:

| Act | Life stage | Level | Example events (Spain) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Arrive** | A1 | landing at Madrid airport with dead signs, getting a SIM card, finding a hostel, ordering your first meal |
| 2 | **Settle in** | A2 | renting a flat, the supermarket, opening a bank account, getting lost on the metro, seeing a doctor |
| 3 | **Make a life** | B1 | a job at a café, making friends, a date, a family dinner, a football match, a job interview |
| 4 | **Belong** | B1–B2 | a wedding, the Feria in Seville, helping a friend through a crisis, a debate with neighbours, telling your own story |
| Finale | **Heart Legend** | B2 | open conversation with the country's greatest legend |

- **Life events are quests** (no combat). They pay out food, coins, friends and
  reputation, and they teach the phrases you'll need in battle.
- **Glitch zones** appear where ANTI is strongest (a corrupted market, a dead train
  station). That's where you fight corrupted legends.
- **Friends** from life events become your party: they help in fights and their
  stories run through each act.

## 8. Where the casual modes fit in the world

- **The Tower** is a training simulation your companion runs inside the phone, built from
  the language data it saved. That's why it works in any language and doesn't change the
  story.
- **Bridge Rush** (in the Tower) trains for the finale, where you have to be the
  bridge between languages: one English phrase, as many languages as you can in time.
- **The Daily Climb** is your companion's daily drill.
