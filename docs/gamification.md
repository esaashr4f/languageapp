# Modes, progression and economy (v3)

Rule behind every system: **what you can say is what you can do.** Every mode feeds the
same thing: your real level in each language.

## 1. Two front doors

### Casual mode: 2–5 minute sessions, no story needed
For people who want a quick, fun daily game.

| Mode | What it is | Session |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily Climb** | 7 prompts, same for everyone, rarer natural answers score more (Krillion loop) | 3 min |
| **Tower** | endless floors in any language or a mix; guardian fight every 10 floors | 2–10 min |
| **Duels** | 1v1 against friends or random players: same prompts, best answers win | 2 min |
| **Party rooms** | 2–50 friends with a room code | 5 min |
| **Masters** | Elite Four + Champion per language; Polyglot League with 3+ crests | 5–10 min |

Appeal: instant start, nothing to remember, shareable scores, leagues, and quick wins.
Works great on a phone in a queue.

### Story mode: 10–20 minute sessions, a full RPG
For people who want a journey: a country campaign per language (see
`story-and-world.md`): life events, glitch zones, legends to restore, friends, a home,
and the Heart Legend at the end.

Appeal: characters you care about, real places and culture, a reason to learn the next
thing, and the feeling of actually living somewhere.

### How they connect
Both modes share **one profile**: the same language levels, spellbook, food bag, coins
and Crests. Casual play makes you stronger in the story; the story unlocks new spells
and legends to use in casual battles. Neither is required, but each makes the other
better.

## 2. Battle system

- Turn-based. An enemy says something in the target language; you reply in time.
- **Your reply is your attack.** The quality of what you say (tiers: Phrasebook ×1,
  Textbook ×1.5, Conversational ×3, Native ×6, Regional ×8.5, Polyglot ×10) sets
  the damage.
- **Spell schools follow grammar:** commands strike, polite requests shield, thanks
  and compliments heal, past-tense storytelling chains combos, idioms are ultimates.
  New grammar unlocks new schools (the subjunctive unlocks one).
- **Restored legends are summons:** call the windmill giant with a Spanish idiom.
- **Weaknesses:** each legend is weak to a skill (formal register, past tenses, slang).
- Missed words become **Glitched words**: weak enemies that come back later until you
  get them right (spaced repetition).

## 3. Food: earned from quests, used as power-ups

Food comes from **life-event quests**, not the shop, so culture and rewards are the same
thing. Help a grandmother at the market and she gives you her tortilla recipe.

- **Dishes** are consumable buffs before or during battles:
  paella (+HP), churros (+5 s per answer), gazpacho (cure a debuff), ramen (+time),
  croissant (shield), tacos al pastor (critical hits).
- **Recipes** let you cook the dish at home from **ingredients** found on quests and in
  markets, so you can make more.
- **Regional specials** come from side quests in each region and are stronger.
- Collecting every dish of a country is a set with its own reward.

## 4. Coins: what they're for

Earned only by playing. Never buys power that money can buy.

- **Ingredients** at local markets (where you also practise bargaining).
- **Your home** in each country: rent upgrades and furniture (decorated with souvenirs).
- **Outfits**, traditional and modern, per country.
- **Travel tickets** to side regions (Canary Islands, Okinawa, Quebec).
- **Hint items:** first-letter hint, peek at the tier list.
- **Companion skins.**

## 5. Progression

- **Language level** per language (A1 to C2), from XP in any mode.
- **Story acts** unlock with level: you can't reach Act 3's job interview until you're
  around B1, which keeps the story honest about your ability.
- **Reputation** per country: speak naturally and locals trust you (side quests, better
  prices, secret areas).
- **Legend collection**, **dish collection**, **Crests**, and the world map turning
  from grey to colour.

## 6. Daily and weekly rhythm

- **Daily:** Daily Climb, one life-event quest per country, Glitched words to clear,
  a streak (freezes earned by playing, never bought).
- **Weekly:** a Master challenge, league results, a rotating event legend.
- **Seasons:** a new country or region, a festival event (Día de Muertos, Hanami,
  Carnival) timed with the real calendar.

## 7. Guardrails

No pay-to-win, no paid loot boxes, one capped opt-in notification a day, a break
suggestion after long sessions, progress never lost.

## 8. Build order

1. **Speaking engine:** grading with tiers (fuzzy match + AI fallback).
2. **Casual:** Daily Climb + Tower in 3 languages (first playable version).
3. **Battle system:** spells, legends, Glitched words, food buffs.
4. **Story: Spain, Act 1** (Arrive): airport, hostel, first meal, first glitch zone.
5. Acts 2–4 and the Heart Legend conversation, then coins, home, outfits.
6. Masters, duels, then the next country.
